Describe the access control that exists, not the portal that doesn't

The document still explained how seven portal pages were wrapped around an
inert template, mounted only after an RPC returned true. That portal was deleted
in 4c7a432, so every reader was being told how a system that no longer exists
works.

Rewritten around the three places access is actually enforced now: restrictive
RLS policies layered on the existing course and ownership rules, the two access
RPCs, and the whitelist check inside each user-facing Edge Function. It also
records why RequireAccess is guidance rather than a boundary -- bypassing it in
the browser still hits the data layer.

Adds the one thing the audit turned up that a reader of this file most needs to
know: static assets are public by design, so anything that must not be public
has to stay out of the build, and problems/ now does.
